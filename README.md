# AI Health Copilot

[![Watch the demo](https://img.youtube.com/vi/tozReWteUAQ/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bQLLBNzaSO8)

**AI Health Copilot** is an intelligent health assessment platform that combines predictive analytics with generative AI to deliver personalized health insights and wellness recommendations.

> Repository: [github.com/Harrypatria/AI_Agent/tree/main/ai_agent_machine_learning](https://github.com/Harrypatria/AI_Agent/tree/main/ai_agent_machine_learning)

---

## What It Does

AI Health Copilot transforms health data into actionable intelligence through:

- **Predictive Health Analytics**  
  Assess risks for diabetes, heart disease, and Parkinson's using validated ML models

- **Intelligent Diagnosis Explanations**  
  Get AI-powered insights into contributing factors and health implications

- **Personalized Wellness Planning**  
  Generate custom diet and fitness plans tailored to your health profile

- **Streamlined User Experience**  
  Clean, intuitive interface designed for effortless health monitoring
